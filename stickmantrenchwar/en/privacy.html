Stickman Trench Wars Privacy Policy

HomePrivacy policySupport

Stickman Trench Wars is a Spino Games game. This policy explains in plain words what happens to your data when you play. The short version: Stickman Trench Wars has no accounts and your progress stays on your device; the game only shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob.

Effective: 2026-11-25

What data is stored

Your progress and settings (the game in progress, completed levels, your best wave, history, service record and chosen language) are saved only on this device, in the app’s local storage. There are no accounts or sign-ups: we never ask for your name, email or any personal information.

What we don’t do

We don’t collect, send or share your progress or settings with Spino Games or anyone else. Spino Games has no servers and no accounts. The only internet connection Stickman Trench Wars makes is the one needed to show the optional video ads (see below).

Tracking and ads

Stickman Trench Wars shows optional rewarded video ads, provided by Google AdMob, that you choose to watch to earn bonus gold, to revive your fort or to borrow a weapon for one run. Nothing is shown unless you tap the button. To show them, AdMob may use the device advertising identifier (IDFA) and similar data (such as device and ad interaction information), but only if you allow tracking when iOS asks (App Tracking Transparency); if you say no, ads can still appear but won’t be personalized. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, a Google consent form asks for your choice before any ad request; you can change it any time in Settings, under Privacy options. Google processes this data under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy. You can also reset or limit the advertising identifier in iOS Settings. Stickman Trench Wars has no other analytics or tracking tools.

Children

Stickman Trench Wars has no accounts and collects no data itself, so it collects none from children either. It has no way to communicate with other people in the game. The optional video ads are the only third-party content; they follow the consent options described above.

How to delete your data

In the game, open Settings and tap Erase progress: this erases the game in progress, completed levels, your best wave, history and service record. You can also remove everything by deleting the app. Since we store nothing outside your device, there is nothing else for us to delete.

Changes to this policy

If Stickman Trench Wars ever changes in a way that affects your data (for example, by adding leaderboards or in-app purchases), we will update this page before releasing the change. The effective date above shows the latest version.

Contact

If you have any question about this policy, write to us at slltechitogames@gmail.com.
