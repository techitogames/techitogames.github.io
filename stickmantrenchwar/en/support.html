Frequently asked questions about Stickman Trench Wars.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Stickman Trench Wars has no accounts and no cloud, so progress is not synced to other devices.

If I delete the app, do I lose my progress?

Yes. Uninstalling Stickman Trench Wars erases everything it saved and it can’t be recovered. Switching phones doesn’t carry your game over by itself either.

How do I start over?

Open Settings and tap Erase progress, then confirm with the red button. Everything is erased and it can’t be undone.

How do I change the language?

In Settings, choose Language. Pick one of the 9 available or leave it on Same as phone.

I found a bug or have a question

Write to us at slltechitogames@gmail.com. Tell us which device and iOS version you use and what happened.
