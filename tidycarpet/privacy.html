Who we are

TidyCarpet is a game by Techito Games. This page explains what data the app uses and why.

What we store

Nothing on our servers. TidyCarpet has no user accounts or sign-up: your games, language and volume settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. We do not use our own analytics tools and we do not collect your name, email, location or contacts.

Rewarded ads

The game shows video ads only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. They are served by Google AdMob. To do so, Google may receive and process data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with the ads. How Google uses this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • European Union, EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: before the first ad you are asked for consent through Google's form. You can change it any time from Settings › Privacy inside the game.
  • iOS: the system asks whether you allow tracking across apps. If you decline you still see ads, just not personalized ones. You can change this in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Even if you never watch an ad, the app initializes the AdMob SDK at launch so a video is ready; the same technical data described above applies in that case.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Since we store no data about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete: what is on your device is under your control. For the data processed by Google, use the consent and tracking controls described above or Google's ad settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. Important changes are announced inside the game.
